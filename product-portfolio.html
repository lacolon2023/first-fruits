What it actually is

One plan for the whole year — not a pile of workbooks

The 365 Day Learning Portfolio is First Fruits' complete daily plan for the K–2 years. It's a full year of Scripture-integrated lessons that weave Bible, reading and language arts, early math, handwriting, and character into one simple daily rhythm. Think of it as the spine of the whole system: it tells you what to teach each day and connects the thematic units and skill packs into a coherent year — so you're never piecing curriculum together again.

Clearing up the name

So, what does “365 Day” mean?

365 days of planned, Scripture-integrated lessons — because discipleship and formation don't take the summer off.

Flexible to your rhythm

Differentiated K–2

The same plan flexes for a kindergartner and a second grader — teach siblings side by side.

Buy once, reuse for years

Common questions

Know exactly what you're getting

Is this a complete curriculum or a supplement?

It's a complete daily core for the K–2 years — Bible, reading & language arts, math, handwriting, and character. Many families use it as their full curriculum; others layer it alongside what they already have. The thematic units add depth in science, social studies, and identity.

What does “365 Day” mean — seven days a week?

It's a full year of daily plans. Use it year-round for daily discipleship and learning, or follow a standard ~180-day school-year pace and use the rest for review and enrichment. It flexes to your family's rhythm.

Does one Portfolio really cover all of K–2?

Yes. It's differentiated, so the same plan flexes from Kindergarten through 2nd grade, and it's reusable year after year and child after child — buy once, teach your whole family.

What subjects does it cover?

Daily: Bible & Scripture memory, phonics & reading, handwriting, early math, and character/social-emotional learning. Science, social studies, and identity themes come through the thematic units that drop into the plan.

Do I need the thematic units too?

No — the Portfolio stands on its own as your daily core. The units are optional depth you can add one at a time as your child grows.

How is $79.99 possible for a full year?

Print-on-demand and digital delivery keep our costs low, and affordability is part of our mission. Because it's reusable across children and years, the cost per child keeps dropping the more you use it.
