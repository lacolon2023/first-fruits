Before you start

What else will I need?

First Fruits is designed to be open-and-go. Here's everything else a typical week calls for — no surprises.

A printer

For the digital version, to print activities and card decks at home. Prefer no printing? The printed Workbook is on the way.

Basic household supplies

Crayons, scissors, glue, paper. Each unit includes a simple, week-by-week materials list.

Specialty supplies

When an activity needs something less common, we include a convenient shopping link — no guessing.

Books

Everything your child needs to learn is included — no separate readers or textbooks required.

A Bible

Bring your family's own. We use a clear, kid-friendly translation and support the versions most families already use.
