Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to serve you, and we never sell your personal information.

Effective date: August 2026

First Fruits respects your family's privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple on purpose: we collect only what we need to serve you, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — such as your name and email address when you join our launch list or request a free sample, and the details needed to fulfill an order (like a shipping address for physical kits).
  • Order information — the products you purchase and basic transaction details. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Basic site information — standard, mostly anonymous data such as which pages are visited, to help us improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the products and free samples you request.
  • To send you launch updates, new-unit announcements, and occasional emails — only if you've asked to hear from us.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To improve our materials and website.

Email and your choices

If you join our launch list, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us. We will honor your request promptly.

Children's privacy

Our materials are made for young children, but our website and email list are intended for the parents, teachers, and leaders who purchase and use them. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the trusted service providers who help us operate — for example, our payment processor, print-on-demand and shipping partners, and email service — and only as needed to serve you, or where required by law.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Just reach out and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business grows. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

Privacy questions or requests? Email us at hello@firstfruitslearning.com and we'll take care of it.

This plain-language policy is provided for clarity and is not legal advice. We recommend reviewing it with a qualified professional before a live commercial launch.
