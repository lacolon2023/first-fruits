A real Grade 1 lesson

Same theme as Kindergarten — 1st-grade academics

Here's Week 1 of Who I Am in Christ at the 1st-grade level. Your Kindergartner and 1st-grader talk about the same big idea — but each does the academics that fit them.

Big idea: I am chosen and created by God.   Memory verse: “I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made.” — Psalm 139:14
Reading & Phonics
  • Silent-e long vowels: made, name, like, time
  • Read the decodable: “God made me to be like Him.”
  • Comprehension (Genesis 1:26–27): Who made people? How are we like God?
  • Retell three things God made
Writing & Language
  • Write two “I am…” sentences — capital & period
  • e.g. “I am made in God’s image. I can show His love.”
  • Memory-verse copywork: Psalm 139:14
  • New words: image · creator · chosen
Math + Faith
  • Add & subtract within 20; number bonds to 10
  • Count & compare: “God knew me before day one”
  • Identity declarations: “Because I’m His, I can…”
  • Hands-on: “wonderfully made” fingerprint art

The Grade 1 curriculum

A full year, leveled up for 1st grade

The same Christ-centered themes as Kindergarten — with 1st-grade content. Built on the Science of Reading, included with your membership.

📖 Reading

Digraphs & blends, long vowels (silent-e), first vowel teams; reading decodable sentences and short passages; growing sight words; retelling a story.

🔢 Math

Addition & subtraction within 20; place value to 120 (tens & ones); measuring length; telling time to the half-hour.

✝️ Writing & Faith

Writing complete sentences, memory-verse copywork, and a child's growing identity in Christ.
