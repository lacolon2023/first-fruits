Why it matters

Character is caught and taught — early

The early years are when children learn how to treat others. Love Like Jesus turns everyday moments — sharing, apologizing, including someone — into teachable ones, building empathy and communication skills on the example of Jesus himself.

What your child will learn

By the end of this unit, your child will be able to…

  • ✓ Define kindness, forgiveness, and empathy in their own words
  • ✓ Practice sharing, taking turns, and including others
  • ✓ Retell a Bible story about how Jesus loved people
  • ✓ Use words and simple steps to work through conflict
  • ✓ Connect the fruit of the Spirit to everyday choices
Big idea: Love one another as Jesus loves us.
Memory verse: “Be kind to one another, tender-hearted, forgiving one another.” — Ephesians 4:32
