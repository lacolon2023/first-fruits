Why it matters

Every child asks: where did I come from?

From their first questions about their body, the world, and how it all began, children are looking for answers. God Made Me meets that curiosity with real, developmentally appropriate science — and the truth that they were made wonderfully, on purpose, by a God who loves them.

What your child will learn

By the end of this unit, your child will be able to…

  • ✓ Name the five senses and describe how each one helps us
  • ✓ Identify major body parts and simple ways to care for their body
  • ✓ Retell the days of creation in order
  • ✓ Express gratitude to God for how they were made
  • ✓ Practice age-appropriate phonics, counting, and observation
