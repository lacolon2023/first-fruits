Why it matters

The world is already telling your child who they are

Long before they can read, children are forming beliefs about their worth — from screens, comparison, and the voices around them. Who I Am in Christ helps you get there first: rooting your child's identity in what God says about them, while building the early reading, writing, and emotional skills they need for everything that comes next.

By the end of this unit, your child will be able to…

How a lesson works

One big idea, taught three ways

Each week centers on a single truth, then meets every child at their level — so a kindergartner and a second grader can learn side by side. Here's Week 1:

Big idea: I am made in the image of God.
Memory verse: “…the new person who was made to be like God, truly good and pleasing to him.” — Ephesians 4:24
Kindergarten
  • Phonics: letter m — “Mighty Maker made me”
  • Creation BINGO & sequencing cards
  • Feelings cards: “When I feel ___, I can go to God for ___”
First Grade
  • Reading comprehension: Genesis 1:26–27
  • Vocabulary: image · creator · special · purpose
  • Writing: “Because I am made in God's image…”
Second Grade
  • STEM: “Mirrors Reflect” experiment
  • We reflect God's character — we are not God
  • Reflection journal + one-paragraph response
