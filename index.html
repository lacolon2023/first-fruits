Common Questions

Know what you are getting before you buy

Clear answers for families, churches, co-ops, and Christian schools.

The launch unit is a focused thematic learning experience. The full 365 Day Portfolio is being built as the broader K–2 system. Product pages should clearly identify which subjects and weeks are included.
The final product should state the default translation and whether alternate verse cards are available. This is a launch decision that should be made before checkout goes live.
Every consumable or specialty item needed for the selected unit, packed by activity. Common household tools such as pencils, scissors, and crayons can be listed separately.
Family purchases should cover one household. Classroom, church, and site-wide reproduction should be governed by the selected license.
Each lesson should include an estimated preparation time, supply list, teaching time, and optional extension so adults can plan quickly.
