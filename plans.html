Common Questions

Know what you are getting before you buy

Clear answers for families, churches, co-ops, and Christian schools.

Each thematic unit — like Who I Am in Christ — is a focused, multi-week learning experience. The 365 Day Portfolio is the complete K–2 system that connects the units with daily phonics, math, reading, and character routines. Start with a unit and build up, or begin with the full portfolio.
We use a clear, easy-to-read translation chosen for young learners, and every unit includes printable memory-verse cards you can send home or post in the classroom.
Every consumable and specialty item needed for the selected unit, packed and labeled by activity. Everyday tools like pencils, scissors, and crayons aren’t included — so you’re only paying for the specialty supplies.
A family purchase covers one household. Classrooms, churches, and multi-site use are covered by our classroom, church, and school licenses — see the plans above.
Every lesson lists an estimated prep time, a simple supply list, teaching time, and an optional extension — so you can plan your day in just a few minutes.
