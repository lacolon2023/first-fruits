Shipping & Returns

Digital delivery is instant; physical kits ship in 2–3 business days — backed by our 30-day guarantee.

Effective date: August 2026

Most of First Fruits is delivered digitally, so you can start right away. The annual Binder ships to your door. Here's how it works.

Digital products

Monthly units, card sets, and other digital lessons are delivered electronically. After checkout you'll receive access to download your files — no shipping required. You can print what you need at home and reuse the files for every child in your care.

The annual Binder

  • Physical items are produced and shipped through our print-on-demand and fulfillment partners.
  • Orders typically ship within 2–3 business days, with delivery times depending on your location and the carrier.
  • Free shipping is available on qualifying orders over $75.
  • You'll receive tracking information by email when your order ships.

Our 30-day satisfaction guarantee

We want your family to feel confident. If a First Fruits product isn't the right fit, contact us within 30 days and we'll make it right.

  • Digital products: because files can't be returned once downloaded, we handle these case by case — reach out and we'll help, whether that's troubleshooting, a replacement, or a refund.
  • Physical items: unused items in resellable condition may be returned within 30 days for a refund of the product price. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, we'll replace it or refund it at no cost to you — just send a photo so we can help quickly.

How to start a return or get help

Email us at hello@firstfruitslearning.com with your order details and how we can help. We're a small, family-run team and we read every message.

Founding-family note: during our launch phase we're especially eager for your feedback. If something isn't working for your family, tell us — it genuinely helps us build better materials.

This plain-language policy is provided for clarity and is not legal advice. Shipping times and terms may vary by fulfillment partner.
