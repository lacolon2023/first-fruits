“Who I Am in Christ” — Unit 1, Week 1

See exactly how First Fruits works: one big idea, taught three ways. The same truth is delivered at each grade’s developmental level — visual, auditory, and kinesthetic — integrating Scripture, academic standards, and hands-on activities into one cohesive lesson.

Big idea: I am made in the image of God.
Memory verse: “Be that new person who was made to be like God, truly good and pleasing to him.” — Ephesians 4:24 (ERV)
Kindergarten
Phonics (Mon–Fri)
Mon m · Tue s · Wed a · Thu t · Fri Review  |  High-frequency words: I, am, the
  • Intro letter m: “Mighty Maker made me” (point up, then to self)
  • Initial-sound thumbs up/down: moon, monkey, sun, mountain…
  • Blending & segmenting: am, mat, map, my
Activities (hands-on)
  • Creation BINGO
  • Sequencing cards for the Creation account
  • Decodable reader: highlight “m” words; heart the high-frequency words
Social-Emotional
  • “When I feel ____, I can go to God for ____.”
  • Feelings picture cards + response cards
First Grade
Reading Comprehension
Read Genesis 1:26–27
  • Before: What does it mean to be made in God’s image?
  • During: Who made people? How are we different from animals? What job did God give us?
  • After: Who do we look like? Should we be kind? Why?
Vocabulary
  • image · creator · special · purpose
Writing Response
  • “Because I am made in God’s image…”
  • I can… / I should… / God wants…
Second Grade
STEM — “Mirrors Reflect”
Question: What does a mirror do?
  • Materials: mirror, flashlight, foil, spoon, aluminum pan
  • Experiment: Which materials reflect best?
  • Discuss: A mirror reflects the person in front of it. God made us to reflect Him — we are not God; we reflect His character.
Reflection Journal
  • How can I reflect God’s love this week?
  • Draw one way · write one paragraph
Whole-child, every learning style. Notice there’s no “worksheet with a verse attached.” Each grade gets the same truth through the right developmental doorway — phonics and play for K, reading and writing for 1st, and hands-on science for 2nd.
